# Chapter 01 - Quick Start for WarriorPlus Affiliates

If you only read one chapter before promoting Trap Defence BTC, read this one.

This is the fast-start version.

The goal is not to understand everything perfectly before you move.
The goal is to start promoting correctly without damaging the offer.

Here is the simplest possible operating flow.

---

## 1. Understand the Offer in One Minute

Do not overcomplicate the stack.

Remember this:

- `Minimal` is the free entry layer
- `Regular` is the main paid offer
- `KIBA` strengthens the paid layer inside `Regular`

That is enough to begin.

If you need a shorter memory hook:

**Minimal gets attention. Regular gets revenue. KIBA increases paid value.**

---

## 2. The Golden Rule

Do not push cold traffic straight into a hard paid close unless they are already asking for it.

The best default route is:

`cold traffic -> Minimal -> deeper interest -> Regular`

Why this works:

- lower friction
- easier trust-building
- less resistance
- better fit for public posts, replies, and short videos

Use the paid link when the person is warm enough to care.

### Mandatory purchase and delivery line

When you describe how the offer works or how the buyer gets access, use this line exactly (or keep it consistent with this meaning):

**Purchase completes on WarriorPlus. Access is delivered by email after payment.**

Do not say checkout is on Whop or that access is granted before payment.

### Where to put each link

- **Public posts and replies:** Use the `Minimal` link only. Do not post the WarriorPlus paid link in open public threads.
- **DM or reply to someone who asked for the paid offer:** Use your WarriorPlus affiliate link when the person has already shown buying intent (e.g. "Where do I buy?" / "What's the paid version?").

This keeps quality high and avoids spamming the paid link to cold audiences.

---

## 3. The First 30 Minutes

If you are starting from zero, do this in order.

### Step 1: Save Your Two Main Links

You need:

- your `Minimal` entry link
- your WarriorPlus affiliate purchase link

Do not start promoting without both ready.

### Step 2: Pick One Audience

Do not target everyone at once.

Choose one of these buyer states:

- traders getting trapped by sharp BTC moves
- exhausted chart-watchers
- FOMO-driven traders who react too fast

One pain is enough.

### Step 3: Pick One Asset

Start simple.

Use one of these:

- a one-line hook from Chapter 09
- a three-line post from Chapter 09
- the sanitized short video script
- a short reply opener

Do not build a full custom funnel before posting once.

### Step 4: Publish One Piece

Your first goal is not perfect branding.
Your first goal is distribution.

Post one clean asset.

### Step 5: Be Ready for the First Response

If someone shows curiosity:

- send the `Minimal` link first
- explain the paid layer only after interest is visible

This one habit will improve your quality of traffic.

## 4. The Default Promotion Sequence

If you are unsure what to do, follow this exact sequence.

### Stage 1: Public Attention

Use:

- one-line hooks
- three-line posts
- short video
- replies to pain-based posts

Goal:

- create recognition
- make the person stop
- move them toward `Minimal`

### Stage 2: Early Interest

When someone engages, asks a question, or clicks through:

- send the `Minimal` link
- use simple defense-first language
- do not drown them in feature lists

Goal:

- get them into the free layer
- let the idea land

### Stage 3: Paid Interest

When someone asks:

- "What is the paid version?"
- "What do I get beyond the free layer?"
- "Where is the full version?"

Then explain:

- `Regular` is the full paid defense environment
- `KIBA` is part of the deeper paid layer
- the paid link is here: `{{warriorplus_affiliate_link}}`

Goal:

- clarify the paid value
- move a warm prospect into the actual offer

## 7. The Three Biggest Beginner Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Sending the paid link too early

If the audience is cold, start with `Minimal`.

### Mistake 2: Explaining everything at once

Do not unload the full architecture in the first touch.

### Mistake 3: Reusing old copy without cleaning it

Do not paste legacy assets containing:

- coupon language
- outdated trial language
- old platform flow wording
- unsupported proof claims

Use the sanitized assets from this guidebook instead.

---

## 8. The Safety Rule

Keep every message aligned with the product identity.

That means:

- defense over hype
- structure over noise
- judgment over certainty
- accuracy over exaggeration

If a line sounds exciting but makes the offer feel fake, do not use it.

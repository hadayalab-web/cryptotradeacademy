# Chapter 02 - Offer Snapshot

This chapter is the one-page explanation of the offer.

If an affiliate asks:

> What exactly am I promoting?

this chapter is the answer.

---

## 1. The Short Version

Trap Defence BTC is a defense-first BTC market offer built to help users avoid traps, wait for clearer advantage, and read structure before reacting emotionally.

It is not strongest when framed as:

- a hype product
- a generic signal room
- a fast-money promise

It is strongest when framed as:

- a BTC defense system
- a structure-first decision environment
- a calmer alternative to reactive trading

---

## 2. What the Buyer Journey Looks Like

At the highest level, the buyer journey is simple:

1. The buyer encounters the defense idea.
2. The buyer starts with `Minimal` or asks for more.
3. The buyer moves into the paid `Regular` environment through WarriorPlus.
4. After purchase, access is delivered through the current post-purchase flow (by email). Any language checkout delivers the full 6-language access pack by email.

For affiliates, the practical rule is:

- use `Minimal` to lower friction
- use `Regular` as the real paid offer
- use `KIBA` only as part of the paid value story

---

## 3. The Offer Stack

The offer is built in three clear layers.

### `Minimal`

Role:

- free entry layer
- first-touch experience
- low-friction introduction to the defense logic

What it does:

- gives the audience a taste of the worldview
- introduces `Trap Score` logic
- helps the audience understand that the real problem is often trap exposure, not lack of activity

### `Regular`

Role:

- main paid offer
- core recurring environment
- the real commercial product affiliates should sell

What it does:

- gives the buyer the deeper defense layer
- adds stronger structure and ongoing context
- creates the main reason for recurring value

### `KIBA`

Role:

- paid-only value layer inside `Regular`
- faster pulse and added context
- premium-value reinforcement inside the paid environment

What it does:

- helps explain why serious users want more than the free layer
- strengthens the paid story without becoming a separate front-end offer

---

## 4. What Makes the Offer Different

The offer is different because it does not lead with more action.

It leads with better restraint.

The strongest differentiation points are:

- it is built around trap avoidance
- it tells users to wait for clearer advantage
- it uses a recognizable decision language such as `Trap Score`, `AVOID`, and `STANDBY`
- it feels like a system, not just market commentary
- it is available across six language lanes

The simplest memory hook is:

**Trap Defence BTC helps users stop reacting to surface noise and start operating with more structure.**

---

## 5. Who the Offer Is Best For

This offer is strongest for people who recognize at least one of these conditions:

- they keep getting trapped by sudden BTC moves
- they feel emotionally reactive
- they overtrade
- they watch charts too much and still feel late
- they want more structure and less noise

This is why the offer is relatively easy to promote.

It speaks to pain people already know.

---

## 6. How the Purchase Flow Works

For public-facing accuracy, affiliates should understand the basic flow clearly.

The simplified flow is:

- buyer sees the offer
- buyer reaches the purchase path
- purchase completes on WarriorPlus
- access is delivered after WarriorPlus payment through the current post-purchase system

Important:

- do not describe checkout as happening on Whop
- do not imply multiple payment routes
- do not imply access is granted before WarriorPlus payment

The purchase story must always stay accurate.

---

## 7. What the Buyer Receives

At the simplest level, the buyer receives:

- the free entry layer through `Minimal`
- the full paid defense environment through `Regular`
- the added paid-only context layer through `KIBA` inside `Regular`

The precise format and pricing details may evolve, so affiliates should always verify current live offer terms before making detailed public claims.

That said, the high-level value proposition stays consistent:

- free layer to enter
- paid layer to go deeper
- premium pulse inside paid

The practical rule for affiliates is:
